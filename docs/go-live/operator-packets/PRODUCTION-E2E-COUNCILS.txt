1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
------------------
This packet defines the complete, real 17-step operational lifecycle for Academic Councils
(Department Councils & College Council). It enforces strict role-based access control,
prohibiting any administrative academic bypass and requiring genuine council member/secretary
actions throughout meeting creation, voting, minutes signing, and decision extraction.

2. REAL OPERATIONAL ROLE ROSTER
-------------------------------
  - Department Council Chair:   department_council_chair
  - Department Council Member:  department_council_member
  - College Council Chair:      college_council_chair (Dean)
  - College Council Member:     college_council_member (Dept Heads & Appointed Professors)
  - Council Secretary:          council_secretary

STRICT GOVERNANCE RULE:
  System Admin (admin) role possesses NO ACADEMIC BYPASS privileges. Admin attempting to
  submit votes, approve topics, or lock minutes without holding explicit council role MUST be
  rejected by RLS and RPC security checks.

3. 17-STEP EXECUTABLE LIFECYCLE SEQUENCE
----------------------------------------

STEP E.1: MEMBERSHIP SETUP & VERIFICATION
  Action: Query `academic_council_members` for active council IDs.
  Assertion: Department Council has 1 active Chair + N active Members. College Council has 1 Chair + N Members.

STEP E.2: MEETING SCHEDULE CREATION
  Action: Call `create_council_meeting` RPC as Council Chair/Secretary.
  Assertion: Meeting created with status `scheduled`, unique meeting_number (e.g. DC-2026-05), scheduled date/time.

STEP E.3: TOPIC INTAKE / SUBMISSION
  Action: Faculty member executes `submit_council_topic` with title, description, attachments.
  Assertion: Topic created with status `submitted`, assigned topic code.

STEP E.4: TOPIC REVIEW & SCREENING
  Action: Council Secretary / Dept Head reviews submitted topic via `review_council_topic`.
  Assertion: Topic status transitions to `approved_for_agenda`.

STEP E.5: AGENDA COMPILATION & PUBLISHING
  Action: Secretary calls `compile_meeting_agenda` -> Adds topics, sets item order -> Calls `publish_meeting_agenda`.
  Assertion: Meeting agenda status becomes `published`. Members receive agenda notification.

STEP E.6: ATTENDANCE LOGGING
  Action: Secretary records attendance via `log_meeting_attendance(meeting_id, member_list)`.
  Assertion: Member presence recorded: `present`, `absent`, or `excused`.

STEP E.7: QUORUM VERIFICATION
  Action: Execute `verify_meeting_quorum(meeting_id)`.
  Assertion: Quorum check evaluates `present_count / total_active_members >= quorum_threshold (0.50)`. Evaluates TRUE.

STEP E.8: SESSION EXECUTION (IN-SESSION TRANSITION)
  Action: Chair calls `start_council_meeting(meeting_id)`.
  Assertion: Meeting status transitions from `scheduled` to `in_session`.

STEP E.9: VOTING ON TOPICS
  Action: Each active present council member calls `cast_council_vote(topic_id, vote_choice)`.
  Choice: `approved`, `rejected`, or `abstained`.
  Assertion: Votes recorded. Admin role casting vote is REJECTED with error `NOT_A_COUNCIL_MEMBER`.

STEP E.10: DRAFT MINUTES GENERATION
  Action: Secretary calls `draft_meeting_minutes(meeting_id, discussions_summary)`.
  Assertion: Minutes record created with status `draft`.

STEP E.11: MINUTES REVIEW
  Action: Council members review draft minutes, submit feedback via `review_meeting_minutes`.
  Assertion: Review notes stored in `minutes_reviews`.

STEP E.12: MINUTES LOCK & SIGNATURE
  Action: Council Chair calls `lock_and_sign_minutes(meeting_id, signature_hash)`.
  Assertion: Minutes status becomes `locked`. Content becomes IMMUTABLE. Post-lock modifications REJECTED.

STEP E.13: DECISION EXTRACTION
  Action: System executes `extract_council_decisions(meeting_id)`.
  Assertion: Official council decisions created with unique decision numbers (e.g. DEC-DC-2026-089).

STEP E.14: ACTION ASSIGNMENT
  Action: Chair/Secretary calls `assign_decision_action(decision_id, assignee_id, deadline)`.
  Assertion: Action items assigned to department heads / staff with target deadlines.

STEP E.15: FOLLOW-UP TRACKING
  Action: Assignee updates status via `update_action_status(action_id, 'in_progress' | 'completed')`.
  Assertion: Progress tracked in decision follow-up ledger.

STEP E.16: ARCHIVING
  Action: Secretary calls `archive_council_meeting(meeting_id)`.
  Assertion: Meeting status becomes `archived`. Read-only access preserved.

STEP E.17: NOTIFICATION & REPORT GENERATION
  Action: System generates council summary report & notifies beneficiaries.
  Assertion: Report generated in Reports Center; notifications sent to department head and dean.

4. PASS CRITERIA
----------------
1. All 17 sequence steps execute cleanly without errors.
2. Quorum check enforced accurately before session start.
3. Voting restricted strictly to authenticated present council members.
4. Admin academic bypass attempts (vote/sign/lock) strictly rejected with 42501 / Access Denied.
5. Minutes locking enforces strict immutability.
